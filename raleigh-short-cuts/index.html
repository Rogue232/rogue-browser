Services

Kept short.
Cut with care.

Raleigh Short Cuts offers three things, all for short hair. If you’re not sure which you need, choose “Not sure yet” on the request form and Shonda will help you decide.

01

Pixies

A pixie is the shortest, boldest version of short hair. Shonda shapes each one around the person wearing it — so it sits right, grows out gracefully, and doesn’t ask much of you in the morning.

  • For first-time pixies and long-time pixie wearers alike
  • Shaped to your face, texture and routine
  • Designed to be low-maintenance day to day
02

Precision
short cuts

Short cuts live and die by their lines. A precision cut is about clean edges, balanced shape and careful detail — a short style that looks deliberate and stays that way between appointments.

  • Cropped bobs, tapered cuts and structured short styles
  • Clean, balanced lines
  • Cut to hold its shape as it grows
03

Restorative
solutions

When short hair needs a reset, a restorative approach focuses on health first. The goal is short hair that feels good, looks healthy and is easy to care for at home.

  • Focused on healthy short hair
  • A plan that suits your hair and your routine
  • Low-maintenance by design
